Rules used

Volume candle (Q1 → Q2 → Q3)
1. Q2's highest volume must be greater than Q1's highest volume.
2. Q2 must close above the Q2 peak volume candle's high (long) or below its low (short). A close inside it is skipped.
3. The Q2 peak volume candle's high–low is the level.
Signal setting: Volume uses volume everywhere below. Volume and delivery needs both Q2 breakouts (volume and delivery above Q1), the level is the Q2 peak volume candle, and the Q3 signal candle must be the highest in both so far; a later day with more volume or more delivery cancels it. Delivery only does the same using delivery quantity and the Q2 peak delivery candle. Days without delivery data can never be a signal when delivery is used.
4. In Q3 the signal candle is a day whose range touches the level and whose volume is the highest of Q3 so far (quarter start to that day). It must be at least "Min days into Q3" trading days after the quarter start, otherwise the first days of a quarter are trivially the peak. It stays the signal only while no later day trades more volume before the trigger; if one does, that earlier candle is dropped and the higher-volume day is checked as the new signal.
Trigger long = a day that closes above the signal candle high (an intraday break is not enough). Entry = the signal candle high, taken on the first later day whose range trades back to that price; stop = the signal candle low. Short is the reverse: close below the low, entry at the low, stop at the signal candle high. If the far side of the signal candle is lost before the entry, the setup is dropped. No trigger or entry by quarter end = no trade. If the entry day also reaches the stop, the stop is assumed hit.
Index confirmation (volume candle strategy): the same Q1 → Q2 → Q3 pattern is run on every index, using index volume. Index has same setup = at least one index the stock belongs to had a Q2 volume breakout over Q1 and closed Q2 beyond its peak volume candle in the same direction. Index already triggered additionally needs that index to have had a signal candle and a close beyond it on or before the stock's entry day. Index groups: sector indices (Auto, Bank, IT, Pharma and so on) or broad / cap indices (Nifty 50, 100, 500, Midcap, Smallcap and so on). Stocks that are in none of the chosen indices are excluded. Membership is today's constituent list, applied to past quarters.
Target = the highest high (long) or lowest low (short) of the lookback bars before the signal candle, or a fixed R multiple. Trades with no room to the target, or below the minimum reward : risk, are skipped.

Level retest (first touch) is the earlier version: the level is the previous quarter's peak volume (or delivery) day, the first touch in the next quarter picks the side from the previous close, and entry is the first close beyond the level; stop is the lowest low made since the touch.

Exit (both) = target or stop, whichever hits first. If both hit on one day the stop wins; gaps fill at the open. Trades still running at the last data day show as OPEN and are left out of the statistics. R = profit divided by the entry-to-stop risk.
No costs, slippage or position sizing. One trade per symbol per base quarter.
