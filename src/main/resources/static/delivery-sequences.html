Delivery Sequences

Every session in the range. RISE = delivery above the previous session; COOL OFF = delivery below the previous session after a rise. Cool High/Low = high / low of the first cool-off day and the candle before it. CoolOffStruc = the first cool-off day against that candle; Cool to Cool = the range against the previous cool-off period. HH = higher high and low, LL = lower high and low, HV = higher high with lower low, LV = lower high with higher low. Green High / red Low cells are the candles that match Cool High / Cool Low. The first session of a period shows (W) (M) (Q) (Y) after its date and gets a line above it: dashed blue = week, green = month, pink = quarter, amber = year (biggest wins), whatever the sort. Blue Traded Qty / purple Delivery Qty = highest in the selected range. Chips under the number: W M Q Y; solid chip = highest of that period, ↑ = first day above the previous period's highest, light chip = breakout only; W blue, M green, Q pink, Y amber. The Period comparison section lists every period. Click a header to sort.
