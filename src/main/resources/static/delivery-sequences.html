Delivery Sequences

Every session in the range. RISE = delivery above the previous session; COOL OFF = delivery below the previous session after a rise. Cool High/Low = high / low of the first cool-off day and the candle before it. CoolOffStruc = the first cool-off day against that candle; Cool to Cool = the range against the previous cool-off period. HH = higher high and low, LL = lower high and low, HV = higher high with lower low, LV = lower high with higher low. Green High / red Low cells are the candles that match Cool High / Cool Low.
